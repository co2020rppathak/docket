What it does for each document

Four jobs that you would otherwise do by hand, one document at a time.

Reads the text

OCR turns a phone photo or a scan into text, trying a second reading when the first one finds no date.

Finds the expiry date

It looks for dates next to words like "valid until" and "expires on", and skips birth, issue and purchase dates.

Files it by category

Insurance, warranty, certificate, medical or ID proof, chosen from the words in the document. You can change any guess.

Watches the deadline

Expired documents and ones expiring within 30 days rise to the top of your dashboard.
